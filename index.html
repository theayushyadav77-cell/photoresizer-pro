Aamtaur Par Pooche Jaane Wale Sawaal (FAQs)

Kya meri photo kisi server ya database me upload hoti hai?

Nahi. Yeh tool 100% Client-Side HTML5 Canvas par chalta hai. Aapki photo phone ke bahar kahin nahi jaati. Aap internet band karke bhi check kar sakte hain.

Kya yeh photo sarkari portal par 100% accept hogi?

Haan, jab aap sahi size (jaise SSC ke liye 20KB se 50KB) aur clear photo upload karte hain toh portal bina kisi error ke accept karta hai.

Kya yeh tool bina internet (offline) kaam karta hai?

Haan! Ek baar page browser me open hone ke baad aap airplane mode on kar sakte hain; saare tools bina rukawat chalte rahenge.

Signature ki photo me peela kagaz safed kaise karein?

"Signature Darkener" tab par jayein aur photo upload karein. Tool automatically kagaz ko pure white (#FFFFFF) aur ink ko dark karke 20KB me download ke liye ready kar dega.
